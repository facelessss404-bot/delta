📋 Admin Officer Portal — Daily Academy Operations
URL: /admin | Login: admin@admin.com
  • Cadet Roster & Enrollment Management Create, update, and manage cadets with roll numbers, assigned squads/batches, and subject curriculum checklists.
  • Daily Parade Attendance Engine (/attendance) Session-based muster. One-click 'Mark All Present/Absent', historical session reopening, and audited record correction.
  • Academics, Marks & Exam Results (/academics) Set up assessments (Written, SSB, GTO, Psych) with maximum scores. Enter marks with strict boundary validation.
  • Physical Video Verification Desk (/physical) Stream cadet-submitted workout videos via short-lived 5-min signed URLs. Enter instructor critiques and click Accept/Reject.
  • Cadet Leave Desk (/leaves) Review cadet applications (medical, emergency). Approve or deny with recorded administrative justification remarks.
  • Notice Board & Training Materials (/notices, /notes) Broadcast urgent alerts (triggers in-app notifications) and upload secure training compendiums to Supabase storage.
Meeting Speaking Script for Admin Demo: "The Admin portal is built for operational speed. During morning muster, the officer selects the subject and can mark the entire batch present in one click. In Physical Verification, videos submitted by cadets are streamed securely via temporary signed tokens—the officer adds critique remarks and approves the drill."

URL: /cadet | Login: cadet@cadet.com
  • Personal Performance Dashboard Interactive percentage rings showing overall attendance (e.g. 90%), sessions attended, academic GPA, and drills passed.
